# Grubclub design rules (learned from review)

**Palette** navy #0E3251, green bean #0F5A5C, kale #1C8859, avocado #9EC878, matcha #8CE39B, kiwi/lime #EEFB71, robin's egg #D7F5FA, blueberry #3FA0BE, plus a complementary orange #F2994A (spicy icon and selected image backgrounds). No yellow. Image backgrounds are flat brand colours, a different one per item.

**Logo** `grub` + `club` + worm. All lowercase. The worm is the real shape (cut from the portfolio logo `grubclub.webp`, used as a mask), never a hand-drawn one. On white: all dark green with a slightly deeper lime worm. On navy: white `grub`, kiwi `club` and worm. The worm sits on the baseline with a small gap from the type.

**Type** Poppins. Body and buttons in Sentence case; the logo stays lowercase. Food item names in Title Case. Site nav and progress labels in ALL CAPS, small, letter-spaced. No widows or orphans.

**Layout** One shared header on every screen (same logo size and position). Checkout pages: header + 3-step progress, discount banner, content with generous margin under the banner, bottom Back/Next bar. Progress steps: About you, Checkout, Select meals; the bar continues from the previous page, never resets. The header, banner and bottom bar do not animate between checkout pages; only the content fades.

**Motion** Calm. Soft fades between scenes, not big colour wipes; no pulsing glows. Backgrounds that are meant to be "already there" (hero leaves, nav) do not animate in. Leaves are flat vector, organic and mismatched, from the brand palette, along the bottom edge only. Loaders use the worm crawling like an inchworm, looping continuously with no gap. Buttons keep their label in the same place across states.

**Components** Selected cards turn lime (no outline). Checkmarks and icons are stroke line icons with rounded ends. Numbered dots are small with space around the number; counters are quiet, not big. Product images are the real photos (pouches) and tubs built to match; mask or cut out cleanly.

**Process** Use the user's portfolio images (kristynagottvald.github.io) for real brand assets. Use "flavour" / "favourite" (Canadian spelling) in body copy unless the user writes otherwise. Default delivery address for demos: 500 Prince Edward Drive, Etobicoke, ON (approximate coordinates); use the visitor's location only if the browser allows it.
