    Common questions

    Questions about Brickanta, answered

    Where do my files stay?

    Where they already are. Brickanta connects to SharePoint, OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox and your project systems, reads from the source and keeps up as it changes. Nothing leaves your environment and nothing trains a model.

    Do we have to change our tools?

    No. Your existing systems stay exactly where they are. Brickanta sits on top of them.

    How do we get started?

    We begin with one live project, one team and a clear goal. Our engineers set it up with your team and your IT, then we roll out to more projects once the first one delivers.

    Does Brickanta make the decisions?

    No, you do. Brickanta reads, links and drafts. You review each draft, and every finding links back to the page it came from.

    Which AI models do you use?

    The same models everyone can buy. We pick the best one for each task, so you’re never locked in and never a generation behind.

    Is it secure?

    Brickanta is ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified and GDPR compliant. Controls, policies and subprocessors are published in our Trust Center.
