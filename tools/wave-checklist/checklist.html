Your 2026 tax checklist
File without fear

Everything to gather before you file your 2026 return with the CRA, for self-employed Canadians and small business owners.

Income
T4, T4A and T5 slips from employers, clients and banks
Invoices and sales records for the whole year
Profit and loss statement (one click in Wave)
Rental, investment and other income records
Business expenses (T2125)
Receipts for every expense, scanned and categorized
Home office: square footage, rent or mortgage interest, utilities
Vehicle logbook and fuel, insurance and repair costs
Meals and entertainment (generally 50% deductible)
Advertising, software, phone and internet
Equipment purchases for capital cost allowance
GST/HST and payroll
GST/HST collected and input tax credits, by period
Business number and GST/HST account number
Payroll summaries and T4s issued to employees
CPP/QPP contributions and instalments already paid
Personal and deductions
RRSP and FHSA contribution receipts
Childcare, medical and donation receipts
Last year's Notice of Assessment
Bank statements reconciled to your books
April 30, 2027Balance owing due for everyone, including the self-employed
June 15, 2027Filing deadline if you or your spouse are self-employed
Year-roundKeep records for six years in case the CRA asks

This checklist is general information, not tax advice. Check canada.ca for current CRA rules and deadlines, or talk to an accountant.
